Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

PantrySnap sells or shares certain personal information (pantry inventory data, shopping trends, coarse demographics, usage analytics) with third parties, as described in our Privacy Policy. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar laws in other states, you have the right to opt out. Opting out never limits what the app does for you.

Three ways to opt out — any one works:

  1. In the app: Settings → Privacy → turn on "Do Not Sell or Share My Data". Takes effect immediately.
  2. Browser signal: we automatically honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC). If your browser sends it, you're already opted out on that device.
  3. Email: nasseriemran@gmail.com with the subject "Do Not Sell My Data" from your account email. We process requests within 15 business days.

You may also designate an authorized agent to submit an opt-out on your behalf (California). We do not require account verification to honor an opt-out, and we will not discriminate against you for opting out.
